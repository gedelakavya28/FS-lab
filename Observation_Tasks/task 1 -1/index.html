About Our Institution

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences (ANITS) is a student-focused institution known for practical learning and industry-ready training.

a prominent autonomous engineering college located in Sangivalasa, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. Established in 2001 by the Anil Neerukonda Educational Society, it is permanently affiliated with Andhra University and approved by AICTE.

Popular Programs

We offer a range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs designed to build real-world skills and leadership qualities.

Undergraduate (B.Tech) Programs

  1. Computer Science & Engineering (CSE): Core and emerging fields like Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science with high seat capacities (240+ intake each).
  2. Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE): Focuses on core hardware, VLSI, and embedded systems.
  3. Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE) & IT: Traditional high-demand branches with dedicated modern labs.
  4. Core Engineering: Civil, Mechanical, and Chemical Engineering.

Postgraduate (M.Tech & MBA) Programs

  1. M.Tech: Advanced specializations including VLSI, Machine Design, and Computer Science streams.
  2. MBA: General management and specialized tracks like Hospital Administration.
